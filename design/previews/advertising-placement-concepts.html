Concept rationale

  • A — Marketplace ListingDirect evolution of the live PublisherCard + hero mockup. Photo-led, calm, conversion-focused — the safest fit in the homepage rhythm (white section, like Marketplace).
  • B — Billboard TicketThe brand’s namesake, literalised: a perforated admission ticket with spec grid, stamp and barcode. Loud, poster-like, sits on the yellow Pricing-style band.
  • C — Ops ConsoleDark control-room treatment on the ink band (like “How it works”). A mono spec readout plus a request→payment→proof chain that visualises the description copy.
